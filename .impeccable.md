## Design Context

### Users
Video editors and reviewers collaborate on high-bitrate original footage, organize projects, exchange timestamped feedback, and track completion on desktop and NAS deployments.

### Brand Personality
Professional, calm, precise. The creator repeatedly asks to retain MarkReel's existing visual language and keep review controls readable and unobtrusive.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use restrained graphite surfaces in dark mode and the original warm yellow-tinted light palette (#f5f4ed backgrounds, #faf9f5 panels). Preserve the shutter logo, orange accent, Inter interface family and three-pane workbench. Preserve the original branded loading sweep animation, with smaller 8px corners. Use the existing mono family for timecodes and changing figures, not decorative English headings. Keep sections unframed, controls compact and media thumbnails as the visual focus. Player overlays must not obscure footage or steal editing interactions; preserve video sizing, fullscreen behavior and markup-toolbar placement.

### Design Principles
- Favor efficient repeated review actions and visible processing state.
- Keep project, media, annotation and reply hierarchy distinct.
- Use existing icons and theme tokens for new controls.
- Reserve stable space for controls; keep scrolling scoped to the intended panel.
- Protect drafts and distinguish reversible actions from permanent changes.

### Design Method
The November 2026 interface refinement follows unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), licensed CC BY 4.0. Its application-specific direction is calm review-room chrome with color carried by real footage and meaningful annotation swatches.
